The Shape Of The Week

A rough itinerary.

Exact days shift with tides, weather, and what the ocean is doing that week — this is the shape of it.

01

Arrival & check-in

Fly into Malé, transfer out to the boat, settle in. Easy first day on purpose.

02

Reef diving, check dives

Get everyone dialed in on gear and buoyancy before the bigger stuff.

03

Manta cleaning station

Early launch for the best light and the calmest water. This is the day people fly here for.

04

Drift diving + sandbank sunset

A proper current dive in the morning, dinner on a sandbank that disappears at high tide.

05

Whale shark search day

Open-ocean snorkel day — no guarantees, which is exactly why it's worth it when it happens.

06

Free day

Dive again, freedive, nap, whatever. First truly unstructured day of the trip.

07

Departure

Slow morning, transfer back to Malé, fly out.

Before You Ask

Frequently asked.

Do I need to be dive-certified?

Open Water certification is required for the diving days; the snorkel/whale-shark days are open to everyone.

What's included?

Accommodation, boat time, guiding, and most meals. Flights and dive insurance are on you — we'll point you to both.

How fit do I need to be?

Comfortable swimmer, comfortable in open water. No technical skill required beyond your dive certification.

When do you run it?

A couple of departures a year, timed to manta and whale shark season. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out with dates.
