Our Story

One founder. One rule.

We don't send you somewhere until we've spent real time there ourselves. Everything else about MO7LEY follows from that.

MO7LEY started the way most good ideas do — as an argument about a bad trip someone else booked. A "luxury" package that turned out to be a resort pool and a shuttle bus. Vlad had spent years doing the opposite: showing up somewhere, staying long enough to actually understand it, and building the trip around what the place was actually good for — not what looked good in a brochure.

So MO7LEY exists to do exactly one thing well: send people to the places we know, to do the things that scare us a little, guided by someone who's already made the mistakes so you don't have to. Motley — because the trip list is a genuinely random, chaotic mix of everything we love. Reef dives one month, alpine ridgelines the next.

How It Started

The short version.

Year 1

The bad trip

A "luxury" booking that was really just a pool and a shuttle bus. The moment we decided we could do better.

Year 2

The Maldives, properly

Weeks, not days. Manta cleaning stations, drift dives, sandbanks with nobody else on them. This became the trip we couldn't stop talking about.

Year 3

Everywhere else

Mountains in three countries, reefs in four, a lot of plane windows in between. The destination list stopped being a bucket list and started being a résumé.

Today

MO7LEY Travel

Group trips, concierge planning, and guided adventures — built only from places we've actually spent time in.

What We Believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

🧭

We go first

If we haven't spent real time somewhere, it doesn't go on the list. No exceptions, no "our partner says it's great."

🫀

Fear is the point

The best trips leave you a little different than you started. We build toward that on purpose.

🌊

Nature comes first

Every itinerary is built around the reef, the ridgeline, or the swell — comfort is designed in around that, not the other way around.

🤝

Still for everyone

Adventure-first doesn't mean exclusive. We tailor intensity — and comfort — to who's actually going.
