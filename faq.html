FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is MO7LEY different from a normal travel agency? +

We only offer destinations we've personally spent real time in — not places "partners" recommend. Every itinerary is built from firsthand knowledge, and it's built around nature and activity first — comfort is designed in around that.

What's the difference between Group Trips and Concierge Planning? +

Group Trips are set departures with a small crew, guided the whole way by Vlad (currently just the Maldives). Concierge Planning is a fully custom itinerary built around your dates, group, and fear-of-choice, for any destination on our list.

What does a "Guided Trip" mean? +

Vlad personally guides your trip on the ground — not just planning it, but being there for it. Available for a limited number of trips per year.

Do you only do extreme stuff? +

No — we lean adventure-first, but we tailor intensity to who's actually traveling. Not every day needs to be a summit push or a deep dive.

How far in advance should I book? +

For the Maldives group trip, as early as possible — seats are limited and seasonal. For concierge trips, 2-4 months gives us the most room to work with.

Can you arrange a boat charter without a full trip? +

Yes — fishing, diving, or just a day on the water. See the Boat Charters page or just ask.
